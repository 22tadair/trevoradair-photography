The photographer

Photos that still feel true years later.

I'm Trevor Adair, a photographer based in Provo, Utah. I photograph weddings, engagements, portraits, family sessions, graduation photos, and corporate events throughout Utah, and I'm happy to travel as far as Farmington for photo work.

What I care about most is simple: photos that look like the people in them, in places that mean something, at moments that mattered. Planning is collaborative, sessions stay relaxed, and the finished photos are ones you'll actually want to share and print.

Trevor Adair Photography is a DBA of Adair Media LLC.
