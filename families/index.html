The session look

Everyone looking — and everyone being themselves.

Yes, we'll get the classic group photo with everyone smiling. But the photos families end up loving most are usually the ones around it: a kid mid-laugh, grandparents holding hands, the whole group mid-conversation.

Sessions are paced around kids and grandparents alike, with breaks built in, so nobody feels rushed through the good parts.

What to expect

From first message to finished photos.

  1. Plan togetherWe pick a location and time together, and talk through group size, outfits, and anything that matters to your family.
  2. The session itselfA relaxed, guided session with room for kids to be kids. Group photos first, candid moments throughout.
  3. Your finished photosYour edited photos are delivered as an online gallery. Delivery details are confirmed when you book.

Planning details

Good to know before you book.

  • Locations: on location across Utah, based in Provo, with travel as far as Farmington for photo work.
  • Group size: from immediate family to extended groups — mention the headcount in your inquiry.
  • Outfits: coordinated, not matching. Pick colors that sit well together and with the location.
  • Kids & pets: welcome, and planned for. Tell us about them ahead of time so the session fits them.

Questions

Family FAQ

Where do family sessions take place?

Family sessions take place on location across Utah. Trevor Adair Photography is based in Provo and travels as far as Farmington for photo work.

What if my kids won't sit still?

That is expected, and it is fine. Sessions are paced around kids, with breaks and play built in, so the best photos often come from the moments between poses.

Can extended family join the session?

Yes. Mention the group size when you get in touch, so the session plan and timing can fit everyone.

How do I book a family session?

Send a message through the contact page with your preferred dates, location ideas, and group size. Availability is confirmed from your inquiry.

How much does a family session cost?

Family sessions: Mini $230, Full $340, Extended $360–$620. Contact Trevor Adair Photography for availability and booking details.
