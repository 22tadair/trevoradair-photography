# Photo Manifest — Trevor Adair Photography

**42 of the 57 image slots now use Trevor's real photos** (November 4, 2026 batch, processed into `assets/photos/`). The remaining 15 slots are still clearly labeled placeholders: every wedding slot (8), both about slots (2), all three journal slots (3), home-service-weddings, and home-about. Every `<img>` alt text on a filled slot describes the actual photo, and every filled gallery `<figcaption>` describes the photo rather than the originally intended location.

When replacing a remaining slot, keep the aspect ratio and update the `<img>` `width`/`height` to the real photo's dimensions (see README.md, "Swapping placeholders for real photos"). Source uploads were small web-sized exports (roughly 350–1060 px on the long edge), so processed files were **not upscaled** — the dimensions below are the real output sizes, smaller than the original slot targets. A full-resolution re-export would sharpen the heroes especially.

Note: the November 4 batch contained no true wedding photographs and no photograph of Trevor himself, so those slots stay placeholders rather than forcing a miscategorized photo. The formal couples with corsages/boutonnières in the batch read as prom, not weddings, and were deliberately not used as wedding photos; two of them (DSC4401, DSC5307) are now used as couple portraits on the Portraits page. Missionary photographs were not placed anywhere on the site. Per Trevor (November 4), the graduation section is now broader than BYU-only: DSC7443 (graduate with a red UTAH sash) and DSC_8646 (graduate in front of a HIGH SCHOOL sign) were placed in graduation-gallery-3 and graduation-gallery-4; the page was renamed from BYU Graduation to Graduation on November 4, 2026.
